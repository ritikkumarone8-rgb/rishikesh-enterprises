Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026 · Rishikesh Enterprises, Hajipur

1. Introduction

Rishikesh Enterprises ("we", "us", "our") operates this website to showcase our electrical products and accept order enquiries from customers in and around Hajipur, Vaishali, Bihar. We respect your privacy and keep data collection to the absolute minimum needed to serve you.

2. Information we collect

  • Order enquiries: when you place an order through the WhatsApp checkout, the message contains only the items in your cart, quantities and totals. You choose what personal details (name, delivery address, phone number) to include.
  • Cart contents: your cart is stored only on your own device in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device except inside the WhatsApp message you approve.
  • We do not require accounts, passwords, Aadhaar, or payment card details on this website. Payments happen via UPI apps, card machines at the store, or cash on delivery / pickup.

3. How we use your information

  • To prepare, confirm and deliver your order within Hajipur and nearby Vaishali areas.
  • To contact you on WhatsApp or phone about your order, delivery slot or warranty service.
  • To improve our stock and offers based on aggregate (non-identifying) interest in products.

4. Cookies & local storage

This website uses no tracking cookies, no advertising pixels and no third-party analytics. A single local-storage key (re_cart_v1) remembers your cart between visits. Clearing your browser data removes it permanently.

5. Sharing of information

We never sell or rent your data. Order details are shared only with:

  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) — solely to transmit the order message you send us.
  • Google Maps — only if you tap "Directions", which opens Maps under Google's own privacy policy.
  • Delivery staff of our own shop, strictly for fulfilling your delivery.

6. Data security

  • This site is designed to run over HTTPS; always check for the padlock before ordering.
  • The page sanitises all stored data and escapes all rendered text (textContent-only rendering) to prevent cross-site scripting (XSS). No third-party scripts are loaded.
  • External links open with rel="noopener noreferrer" to protect you from tab-nabbing and referrer leakage.
  • No payment card numbers or UPI PINs are ever requested or stored by this website. Never share an OTP or UPI PIN with anyone, including us.

7. Children's privacy

Our products and services are intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 18.

8. Your rights

You may ask us at any time to view, correct or delete any personal information we hold about your orders (for example WhatsApp chat history at our shop phone). We will comply within 7 days. Call or WhatsApp us at +91 94301 22345.

9. Data retention

Order records are kept for a maximum of 12 months for warranty and billing purposes, then deleted. Your cart on this device remains until you clear it or your browser data.

10. Changes to this policy

Any updates will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Continued use of the site after changes constitutes acceptance.
