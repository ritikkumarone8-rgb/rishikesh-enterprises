Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 September 2026 · Rishikesh Enterprises, Yadav Chowk, Hajipur

1. Introduction

Rishikesh Enterprises operates this website to showcase products and accept order enquiries in and around Hajipur, Bihar. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to serve you.

2. Information we collect

  • Orders: when you confirm an order we store the order code, items, quantities, prices, coupon used and totals, plus the optional name/phone you provide, in our Supabase database so the store can fulfil it.
  • WhatsApp: order messages are sent only when you choose to open WhatsApp, or automatically to the store if the store has enabled the WhatsApp Business API integration.
  • Location (optional): computed on-device only to show distance from the store; never stored or shared.
  • Cart: stored only in your browser's local storage.

3. How we use it

To prepare, confirm and deliver orders, and to contact you about them. We never sell or rent your data.

4. Security

The database is accessed with a public key protected by Row Level Security; order writes are insert-only for visitors. No payment cards, UPI PINs or OTPs are ever requested.
