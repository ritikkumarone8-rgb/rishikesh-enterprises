Privacy Policy

Last updated: 9 September 2026 · Rishikesh Enterprises, Yadav Chowk, Hajipur

1. Introduction

Rishikesh Enterprises ("we", "us", "our") operates this website to showcase our electrical products and accept order enquiries from customers in and around Hajipur, Vaishali, Bihar. We respect your privacy and keep data collection to the absolute minimum needed to serve you.

2. Information we collect

  • Order enquiries: when you place an order through the WhatsApp checkout, the message contains only the items in your cart, quantities and totals. You choose what personal details (name, delivery address, phone number) to include.
  • Location (optional): if you allow location access, we compute your approximate distance from our Yadav Chowk store entirely on your device to show "Our store is at ~x km". Coordinates are never stored, transmitted or shared.
  • Cart contents: your cart is stored only on your own device in your browser's local storage.
  • Product data: catalogue information is loaded read-only from our Supabase database using a public anon key. No customer accounts exist.
  • We do not require accounts, passwords, Aadhaar, or payment card details on this website.

3. How we use your information

  • To prepare, confirm and deliver your order within Hajipur and nearby Vaishali areas.
  • To contact you on WhatsApp or phone about your order, delivery slot or warranty service.

4. Cookies & local storage

No tracking cookies or analytics. A single local-storage key (re_cart_v1) remembers your cart; a session flag remembers the location prompt.

5. Sharing of information

We never sell or rent data. Shared only with WhatsApp (your order message), Google Maps (directions), Supabase (read-only catalogue; no personal data), and our own delivery staff.

6. Data security

  • HTTPS recommended; database accessed with public anon key protected by Row Level Security (read-only for visitors).
  • All rendered text is escaped (textContent-only) to prevent XSS; external links use rel="noopener noreferrer".
  • No payment card numbers or UPI PINs are ever requested or stored here.

7. Children's privacy

Services intended for adults; no knowing collection from children under 18.

8. Your rights

Request view/correction/deletion of order information any time; we comply within 7 days. Contact +91 91991 56794.

9. Data retention

Order records kept max 12 months, then deleted.

10. Changes to this policy

Updates posted here with a new "last updated" date.
